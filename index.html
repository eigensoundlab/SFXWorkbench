        Output
        Finishing

        Finishing is part of the output you hear and applies to everything you save: output, variations, slices and loops.

        Effects run in order, top to bottom, on the selected sound. Double-click a slider to reset it.

          Reads the structure of the selected sound (attack, body, tail, transients, pitch and brightness) and adds accents where they belong: a click before the shot, a sub under the peak, a slap or debris after the tail, a ring on every transient. Accents are tuned to the sound and follow its loudness.

          Variations can add these accents too, slightly different in every file.

          Both tools use the selected source sound without the effect chain. Add the output to the library first if you want effects included.

          Split takes

          Record many takes in one file (footsteps, clicks, impacts) and split them into separate sounds at the silences.

            Seamless loop

            Select the steady part of a hum, engine or ambience on the waveform. The loop is crossfaded with the audio just before it so it keeps its length. Start / loop / stop gives you the three files a looping device needs.

            Everything runs in this browser tab. No audio is uploaded anywhere.
